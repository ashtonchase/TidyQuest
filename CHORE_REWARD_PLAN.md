# Part 3: Reward System

## Philosophy

- Rewards should feel **special and earned**
- Mix of **immediate small wins** and **big goals**
- All redemptions require **parent approval**
- Ages 4 and 8 have different reward preferences

## Coin Pacing

- **Aurora**: Earns enough for 1-2 small rewards OR saving for 1 big reward per week
- **Cooper**: Earns enough for 1 small reward OR saving for 1 medium reward per week

This creates natural motivation:
- Aurora can choose between immediate reward or big saving goal
- Cooper builds toward tangible rewards while enjoying small wins

# Implementation Notes

### For Parent

1. **Start slow** — Replace 2-3 tasks per week, not all at once
2. **Cooper needs help** — Expect to assist with 50% of Cooper's tasks initially
3. **Celebrate streaks** — Kids this age love seeing progress visually
4. **Adjust down if needed** — If either child is overwhelmed, reduce frequency

### Shared Tasks Clarification

| Task | Who Does It |
|------|--------------|
| Cat Litter | Aurora ONLY |
| Cat Feeding | Both can help (same or alternating) |
| Make Bed | Both (independent after taught) |
